Decision (design): Use one shared MLX-VLM process with on-demand switching between DeepSeek-OCR-2 and NuExtract3. This supersedes DECISION-20260814-134657-864956's simultaneous-loading interpretation because upstream mlx-vlm keeps only one text-generation model resident. A multi-resident model cache is rejected due to its upstream incompatibility and unnecessary memory cost.
