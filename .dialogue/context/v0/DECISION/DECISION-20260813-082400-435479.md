Decision (design): Chandra adoption requires a representative, category-mapped distributable corpus and comparative results against Tesseract, GLM-OCR, and NuExtract3. Record inputs, prompts, raw outputs, latency, failures, fidelity, memory, and qualitative layout review; do not automatically adopt against a numeric threshold. Rationale: a compatibility smoke test and synthetic sanity corpus prove only transport and scoring, not real document OCR quality. Rejected alternatives: adopting from a single smoke test, synthetic-only scores, or an arbitrary automatic fidelity threshold.
